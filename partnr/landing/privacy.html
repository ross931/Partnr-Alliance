This Privacy Policy explains how Partnr LLC ("Partnr," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website, platform, and related services (collectively, the "Service"). Partnr LLC is a company registered in Idaho, United States.

By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

If you have any questions, contact us at ross@getpartnr.co.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, company name, and a password (passwords are stored in hashed form by our authentication provider; we never see your plain-text password).

Partnership diagnostic information. When you complete our partnership diagnostic, we collect your name, work email, company name, and the answers you provide about your cloud partnership. We use this to generate your results and to follow up with you about Partnr.

Payment information. When you subscribe to a paid plan, payment is processed by our third-party payment provider, Stripe. We do not collect or store your full credit card number. We receive limited billing details from Stripe, such as your subscription status and the last four digits of your card.

Content and partnership data. We collect the information you enter into the platform — such as partner records, programs, certifications, activities, contacts, and notes — and information you choose to import.

Connected integration data. If you choose to connect a cloud partner account (for example, AWS Partner Central), we access certain partnership data from that account on a read-only basis, using credentials you provide. We only access data necessary to provide the Service, and we never modify data in your connected accounts.

Usage information. We collect basic technical information about how you use the Service, such as log data, device and browser information, and actions taken within the platform, to operate and improve the Service.

Communications. If you contact us directly, we keep a record of that correspondence.

We use the information we collect to:

  • Send you service-related communications, including diagnostic results, account notices, and follow-up about Partnr;
